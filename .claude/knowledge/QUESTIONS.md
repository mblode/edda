# Interview questions — Blode.md

Open-ended. Do not mention Blode.md’s editor refusal, $0, or Mintlify in the question unless she names them first. One answer per question. Use on the next signups; stop when the answers stop surprising you.

**Q1.** Walk me through how the last feature you shipped got its docs onto the public internet. [H1, H2]
→ follow-ups, same story: Where did you write the words? Who reviewed them? What published them?

**Q2.** What do you use for docs today? When did you pick it? [H2]
→ follow-ups: What else did you look at? What made you stay?

**Q3.** What happened in the last couple of weeks that made you look at a docs host (or at Blode.md)? [H3]
→ follow-ups: Had that happened before? What did you do then?

**Q4.** Have you ever paid for a docs host or a docs tool? How much, and what happened? [H4]
→ follow-ups: Whose budget? What would have to be true for you to pay again?

**Q5.** If you could change one thing about how you publish docs, what would it be? [H5]
→ follow-ups: Why that, not something else? Have you tried to change it?

**Q6.** When you talk to a teammate about this, what do you call the problem? [H6]
→ follow-ups: Say the last sentence you actually said to them, as close as you remember.
